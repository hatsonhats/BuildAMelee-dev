BuildAMelee v{version}
{underline}

Build your own fighter in Melee: put any character's specials, aerials,
jab, dash attack, tilts, smash attacks and throws on any other character,
then play locally or on Slippi Online.

INSTALL
-------
You need Slippi and a clean NTSC v1.02 Melee ISO.

Windows:     Drag your Melee ISO onto "DRAG VANILLA MELEE HERE.bat".
Linux/Mac:   ./patch_linux_mac.sh "path/to/Melee 1.02.iso"

This creates BuildAMelee.iso in this folder. In the Slippi Launcher
settings, set it as your Melee ISO.

To play online, both players need the same BuildAMelee version.

CONTROLS
--------
On the character select screen, pick a character and press Z to open
the build panel.

  Up / Down        Choose a move slot
  Left / Right     Change the move in that slot
  L / R            Previous / next tab
  X                Randomize every slot
  Y                Reset the slot
  A                Next slot / press the selected button
  Start, B or Z    Lock in and close
