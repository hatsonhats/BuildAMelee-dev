# BuildAMelee v1.3.6

## Borrowed moves
- Peach's down smash now brings her dress: whoever borrows it spins in Peach's dress, sized to fit their waist and body, hem on the floor and flaring out as the move plays.
- Mr. Game & Watch's down special fills and fires when borrowed (it caught projectiles without filling); Ness's PSI Magnet heals the same way.
- Mr. Game & Watch's key, box, pan, bucket, Judge hammer and other items are black instead of white on whoever borrows them, and the Judge number faces the screen.
- Swords, tails and props from borrowed moves (Marth's and Roy's swords, Mewtwo's tail, Peach's dress and items, Mr. Game & Watch's props, Kirby's stone and more) now load a trimmed model of just those parts, a fraction of the memory, so they show up even in full matches instead of falling back to a Beam Sword or disappearing.

## Known issues
- Ice Climbers borrowing Ganondorf's or Bowser's side special do not grab.
- Sheik's chain is the wrong length on some characters.

## Install
Unzip, drag your clean Melee 1.02 ISO onto `DRAG VANILLA MELEE HERE.bat` (Linux/Mac: `patch_linux_mac.sh`), and play the new `BuildAMelee.iso`. Online play and replays need the same BuildAMelee version on both sides.
