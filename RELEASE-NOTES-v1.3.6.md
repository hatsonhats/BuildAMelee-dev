# BuildAMelee v1.3.6

## Borrowed moves
- Peach's down smash now brings her dress: whoever borrows it spins in Peach's dress, flaring out as the move plays.

## Known issues
- Ice Climbers borrowing Ganondorf's or Bowser's side special do not grab.
- Sheik's chain is the wrong length on some characters.
- In very full matches, Marth's and Roy's swords can show as a Beam Sword, and Peach's dress may not appear (not enough memory for the donor's model).

## Install
Unzip, drag your clean Melee 1.02 ISO onto `DRAG VANILLA MELEE HERE.bat` (Linux/Mac: `patch_linux_mac.sh`), and play the new `BuildAMelee.iso`. Online play and replays need the same BuildAMelee version on both sides.
