# BuildAMelee v1.3.8

## Borrowed moves
- A borrowed move's weapon, tail or prop and its hitboxes are never smaller than on the original character. A smaller fighter borrowing a bigger one's move (Jigglypuff with Marth's sword, Pichu with Bowser's moves) now gets the full-size version instead of a shrunken one. Bigger fighters still get a larger version, as before.
- Link and Young Link borrowing Marth's or Roy's moves now swing the borrowed sword (their own stays in their other hand).
- Mr. Game & Watch with a borrowed down tilt keeps using it when he repeats it quickly, instead of switching back and forth with his own.

## Character select
- The BuildAMelee version and build are always shown in the bottom left, even with the build menu open. Two players need the same build to use their builds online.
- Starting a Slippi search and backing out no longer makes the build menu disappear.

## Known issues
- Ice Climbers borrowing Ganondorf's or Bowser's side special do not grab.
- Sheik's chain is the wrong length on some characters.

## Install
Unzip, drag your clean Melee 1.02 ISO onto `DRAG VANILLA MELEE HERE.bat` (Linux/Mac: `patch_linux_mac.sh`), and play the new `BuildAMelee.iso`. Online play and replays need the same BuildAMelee version on both sides: v1.3.8 cannot exchange builds with earlier versions.
